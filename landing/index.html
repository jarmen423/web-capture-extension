Frequently asked questions

Is Web Capture Pro really free? ▼
Yes! The core features — viewport screenshots, text extraction, auto-navigation, and HTML/Markdown export — are completely free and unlimited. Pro unlocks full-page mode, batch URLs, true PDF generation, and custom selectors.
Does it work on single-page apps (SPAs)? ▼
Partially. Auto-navigation works best on traditional multi-page documentation sites. For SPAs like React or Vue docs, we recommend using Batch URL mode where you paste the exact URLs you want to capture.
Is my data private? ▼
Completely. All capture and processing happens inside your browser. No data is sent to external servers, cloud APIs, or our infrastructure. Your screenshots and extracted text never leave your machine.
What browsers are supported? ▼
Chrome 109+ and Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Opera). True PDF generation requires Chrome 109+ for offscreen document support. Firefox support is on the roadmap.
Can I get a refund? ▼
Yes. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all Pro subscriptions. If Web Capture Pro doesn't meet your needs, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.
